ABOUT 50 ON GUGS

Mr.
Sindile “Sinbad” Kama

Founded by Sindile “Sinbad” Kama , 50 on Gugs is a celebration of Gugulethu hospitality, great food, and local culture. A passionate entrepreneur, avid golfer, and co-founder of Sinbad Wines, Sinbad built 50 on Gugs to be more than just a tshisanyama—it is a community hub famous for its signature SAUSAGE PIZZA, where friends, families, and sports fans gather to feel right at home.
